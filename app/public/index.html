Designed for readers who lose focus easily

  • Bottom line first. One short sentence, then a checklist of what to do and by when. Details stay folded away.
  • Focus mode hides everything else and enlarges the text. Listen reads the answer aloud using your browser's own voice.
  • Nothing made up. Every sentence and every step must quote the club's current documents, and the server checks each quote word for word. If a deadline is not in the quoted text, it is flagged. If nothing can be verified, the answer is withheld.
  • Asks instead of guessing when a question is ambiguous, and says so plainly when the documents don't cover something.

Design guided by the W3C guidance Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities. This is not a medical tool and does not ask about or record any health information.
